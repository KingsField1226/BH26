  Fix #1: export LDFLAGS="-Wl,-rpath,$CONDA_PREFIX/lib $LDFLAGS"
  Cleanup: `cd deps/sparsehash && git clean -xfd` (removed corrupted
  autotools state: compile, config.log, configure~, src/config.h.in~,
  test-driver).
